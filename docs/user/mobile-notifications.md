# Mobile notifications

Turn on **Phone notifications** in **Settings → Notifications** to get an alert when an agent finishes, needs you, hits a usage limit, or resumes after one. Tap a notification to open its thread. Each computer you have paired sends its own notifications, so it must be running; there is no account to sign in to and no hosted service in between.

Notification permission and Android notification channels are controlled in system Settings. Force-stopping the Android app in system Settings prevents delivery until you open it again.

Android delivery needs your own Firebase setup and EAS project; see [ViewCode Android app](./android-app.md#notifications).
